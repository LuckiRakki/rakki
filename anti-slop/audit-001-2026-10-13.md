# antislop audit 001 (2026-10-13)

Mode: after (audit first; nothing changed until numbers are approved). Scope: Rakki desktop
(every screen and state) and the phone's new Devices screen. Direction: the design ethos in
docs/DESIGN_PASS.md (Spotify x Feishin, Spicy Lyrics first, customizable, light, one Rakki,
real icons, plain words, private, no loading screens).

Design Read: a desktop music player for its owner, in a Spotify x Feishin language driven by
album-art colour, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

Evidence: headless Chrome screenshots at the smallest window (900 x 600) and at 1440 x 900, the
antislop contrast checker, and code inspection. Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate =
HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW.

## HIGH

1. **Narrow window breaks every page** (R-03). At the smallest window (900 px) with both side
   panels open, the main area is about 200 px wide: Settings wraps word by word, the album page
   shows a blank grey block, Home's Customize button is cut off. Fix: real width states (the
   right panel becomes an overlay or closes under about 1180 px; the sidebar narrows to icons
   under about 1000 px), and a minimum width for the main area.
2. **Player bar overlaps at narrow widths** (R-03). At 900 px the transport buttons sit on top
   of the right-hand controls (repeat over the sidebar button) and the seek bar shrinks to a
   stub. Fix: the bar gets its own width states; less-used buttons move into a menu when tight.
3. **Spicy lyrics run off the edge at narrow widths** (R-03). The engine sizes text from the
   window width, not its column, so at 900 px lines are cut off on the right. Fix: size the
   lyrics from the column they sit in.
4. **Lucid accent can be unreadable** (R-25). Lucid keeps the album colour's lightness at
   58-68% but lets saturation reach 100%, so a strong blue (#2929ff) is 2.54:1 as accent text on
   the dark surface, and black text on an accent button is 2.86:1. Fix: adjust the accent until
   it measures 4.5:1 against the surfaces, and pick black or white text per accent.
5. **Genre tiles: white text on yellow and green tiles** (R-25). Tiles use 42% lightness, which
   gives 1.56:1 (yellow) and 1.96:1 (green) for the white genre names. Shared with the phone app.
   Fix: darken each tile until white text measures 4.5:1.
6. **Muted text on hovered and selected rows** (R-25). --text-3 #85858d is 4.12:1 on --surface-3
   (hovered rows, pills, Devices buttons) and 3.43:1 on --surface-4. Fix: a slightly lighter
   muted grey, checked against every surface it sits on.
7. **"Live" pill** (R-25). White on #e5534b at 11 px is 3.70:1. Fix: a darker red, measured.
8. **Seek and volume bars are mouse-only** (R-32). The player bar, the video bar, the remote
   bar and the mini player's bars can't be focused or moved with keys, and aren't announced as
   sliders. Fix: slider role and values, arrow keys, Home and End.
9. **Some clickable things ignore the keyboard** (R-32). Recent searches (focusable, but Enter
   does nothing), radio station cards (no keyboard at all), and song table rows (no row focus;
   select and play are mouse only). Fix: Enter and Space on all of them; arrow keys move
   through a song table's rows.
10. **Right-click menus are mouse-only** (R-32). No key opens them (Menu key, Shift+F10), focus
    stays behind when they open, and arrow keys don't move through items. Fix: all three.
11. **Focus that can't be seen, a popover Escape ignores** (R-32, R-26). Sort dropdowns have
    their focus outline removed with nothing in its place; find boxes only show a faint
    background change; the Lyrics timing panel doesn't close with Escape. Fix.
12. **Sidebar resize handles are mouse-only** (R-32). Fix: focusable, arrow keys resize.
13. **No error states** (R-27). The desktop treats itself as always online: if the server can't
    be reached or a request fails, pages render empty or blank with no message. Fix: an offline
    bar like the phone's, a short message with Try again on a failed page, and cached content
    kept on screen.
14. **Missing empty states** (R-27). Liked Songs with no likes, an empty playlist, Albums /
    Artists / Playlists with none, and Home before anything has played all render blank. Fix:
    one line saying why it's empty and the one action that fills it.
15. **Phone Devices screen: small tap targets** (R-03). "Play here" / "Play there" are about 30 px
    tall; the transport icons reach about 38-42 px with their slop. Fix: 44 px minimum.

## MEDIUM

16. **Uppercase, widely tracked labels** (R-06). "MUSIC VIDEO" (0.12em), the queue table's column
    headings, "CONTROLLING", "LIVE", "UP NEXT IN 5". Fix: sentence case, normal spacing.
17. **Two icon families** (R-04, ethos 5 "one Rakki"). The desktop uses Lucide (the default
    library look); the phone uses Ionicons, so the same actions look different on each.
    Decision: move the desktop to the phone's icon family, or keep Lucide with a written reason.
18. **Shadows on every card** (R-12). 22 shadow rules, including every grid card's artwork, so
    the grids float. Fix: keep shadows on things that sit above the page (menus, popovers, the
    full player's cover, the video); grid cards stay flat, with the hover lift as the cue.
19. **Spicy dimmed lines are below 4.5:1 by design** (R-25 against the Spicy look; R-37 asks the
    owner). Unsung lyric lines and the sidebar card's upcoming lines are dimmed on purpose
    (about 3.8:1 on a dark tint), as in Spicy Lyrics. Decision: keep the Spicy look as an
    approved exception, or raise the dim floor to 4.5:1.
20. **Actions that wait** (ethos 9, no loading screens). Add to playlist ("Saving" before it
    closes; rows show "Checking..."), Lyrics timing's Save to server, Play here / Play on (busy
    note), playlist rename / picture / delete (wait for the server), Radio's Add ("Adding..."),
    Last.fm's Check. Fix: finish on screen at once, then "Added to Wednesday" / "Couldn't add",
    undoing a failed change.
21. **Spinners with no words** (R-27). Eight bare spinners. Fix: keep what was on screen while
    refreshing; where a first load really waits, say what's loading.
22. **Glass header in the queue table** (R-10). One blurred sticky header. Fix: a solid header
    tinted from the background (the dose is fine; it adds little).

## LOW

23. **Pills nearly everywhere** (R-11). 16 pill-shaped rules: primary and secondary buttons,
    filters, Devices buttons, segmented controls, the Update button. The ethos says pills for
    chips. Decision: pills for chips and toggles, an 8 px radius for action buttons; or keep
    pills (Spotify's convention) with that reason written.
24. **Accent in too many places** (R-29, "one deliberate accent"). The accent marks playing
    state, hearts, tags, toggles, slider hovers, the drop line, focus rings, the Update button
    and more (35 rules). Fix: accent for what's playing and the main action; neutral elsewhere.
25. **Dark only** (R-21). Reason to record rather than change: Rakki's direction is Spotify x
    Feishin with art-driven colour, on both apps.
26. **Design reasons aren't written down** (R-31). Radii, shadows, motion timings and Inter have
    no recorded reasons. Fix: a short DESIGN.md for Rakki (the ethos plus the tokens and why).
27. **Code** (refactor). layout/frame.tsx, pages/pages.tsx and styles.css are large and mixed;
    some leftovers (unused styles from replaced views). Fix: split by area, remove leftovers, and
    an antislop-code comment pass. No behaviour changes.
